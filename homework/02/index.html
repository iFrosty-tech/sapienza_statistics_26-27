§1 Problem statement

The assignment reads, verbatim:

HMWK 2 — From Wallet Seed to Ethereum Address: Hashing, Key Derivation and Cryptographic Primitives. Statistical properties. TASK: Check the practical cryptographic pipeline used in Ethereum wallets, from a mnemonic phrase to an Ethereum address and briefly describe the role and purpose of the following standards and cryptographic primitives: BIP-39 — mnemonic sentence and seed generation; BIP-32 — hierarchical deterministic (HD) key derivation; BIP-44 — standard derivation paths for hierarchical deterministic wallets; secp256k1 — elliptic-curve public-key cryptography; Keccak-256 — hash function used in Ethereum address derivation; EIP-55 — mixed-case checksum encoding for Ethereum addresses.

The questions addressed and the structure of the work are stated here.
